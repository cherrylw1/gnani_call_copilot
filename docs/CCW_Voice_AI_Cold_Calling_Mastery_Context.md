4. **Sovereign Stack Control:** Zero 3rd-party model renting; 100% data privacy and enterprise security compliance.

## 3. Strict User Directives & Strategic Principles (Sharath's Mandates)

Throughout the iteration process, Sharath established strict, non-negotiable rules for cold calling success:

### ❌ **What Is Completely Banned & Rejected:**
* 🚫 **No Samsung Claim:** Explicitly removed all mentions of Samsung backing (not 100% accurate).
* 🚫 **No Canned SDR Openers / Gimmicks:** Banned overused SDR lines such as:
  * *"This is a cold call, you can hang up or give me 30 seconds..."*
  * *"Are you looking to implement Voice AI?"* (prospect feedback proved this sounds way too casual and annoying).
  * *"Did I catch you at a bad time?"*
  * *"I noticed on LinkedIn that you lead contact center operations..."* (sounds robotic and fake).
* 🚫 **No Blanket BPO Assumptions for Executive Roles:** Never assume a COO, CIO, or CX VP works at a BPO or cares about "BPO costs". A property management COO cares about resident maintenance; a utility COO cares about storm outage call spikes; a credit union COO cares about member banking.

### ✅ **What Is Mandatory:**
* ✔️ **Use "CCW" as the Shared Event Anchor:** Introduce ourselves naturally as following up from CCW where we showcased our native voice-to-voice models.
* ✔️ **Explain WHAT the Company Actually Does:** The teleprompter must immediately tell Sharath what the prospect's company does (e.g., American Homes 4 Rent = 60,000+ single-family rentals; Sacramento County/SMUD = resident billing & outage calls; MIT FCU = member banking calls; Kohl's = retail tracking).
* ✔️ **Direct, Casual, Peer-to-Peer Executive Tone:** Sound like a confident enterprise account executive reaching out with a legitimate business reason.
* ✔️ **Substantive, Elevated Elevator Pitch:** Pitch must provide real technical substance (sub-200ms latency, ElevenLabs + Vapi model comparison, live agentic execution, hard ROI metrics) rather than a weak 2-line summary.
