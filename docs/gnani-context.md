# gnani.ai Context

gnani.ai is an enterprise Voice AI company.

For this project, treat gnani.ai as a company that helps businesses automate, improve, and analyze high-volume customer conversations, especially phone calls.

The core business value is not simply "AI"; it is contact-center and customer-operations improvement.

gnani.ai helps businesses with:

- AI voice agents
- Enterprise voice automation
- Agent assist
- Conversation analytics / speech analytics / QA analytics
- Voice biometrics and authentication
- Workflow automation
- Customer support automation
- Outbound and inbound customer conversations
- Human handoff with conversation context
- Multilingual and production-grade speech AI

## What I am selling

I am not trying to sell a generic chatbot.

I am selling business outcomes such as:

- reducing repetitive call volume
- reducing contact-center cost
- improving agent productivity
- improving average handle time
- improving QA visibility
- analyzing more customer conversations
- improving compliance visibility
- automating repetitive inbound/outbound workflows
- improving customer experience
- supporting collections, support, enrollment, renewals, reminders, and follow-up workflows

## Main product angles

### 1. AI Voice Agents

AI voice agents can handle repetitive voice conversations, collect information, answer common questions, route calls, schedule callbacks, qualify leads, support customers, and hand off to human agents when needed.

Best-fit use cases:

- customer support
- appointment scheduling
- lead qualification
- payment reminders
- collections follow-up
- policy renewal
- claims status
- order status
- returns/refunds
- surveys
- onboarding
- customer engagement

### 2. Agent Assist

Agent Assist helps human agents during live calls.

It can provide:

- real-time suggestions
- next-best actions
- compliance reminders
- account/context guidance
- response suggestions
- post-call summaries
- faster documentation
- coaching insights

Best-fit use cases:

- contact centers
- BPOs
- healthcare enrollment teams
- insurance support
- financial services
- complex customer-service workflows
- new agent ramp-up
- QA and coaching

### 3. Conversation Analytics / QA Analytics

Conversation analytics helps teams understand what is happening across customer calls.

It can help with:

- QA coverage
- sentiment tracking
- compliance checks
- risk flagging
- coaching opportunities
- root-cause analysis
- customer pain discovery
- agent performance improvement

The pitch is: instead of reviewing only a small sample of calls manually, businesses can analyze customer conversations at scale.

### 4. Voice Biometrics

Voice biometrics helps businesses authenticate callers using voice.

It can help with:

- caller verification
- passive authentication
- fraud reduction
- faster authentication
- reduced dependence on security questions
- better customer experience

## Cold call positioning

The app should help me sound like this:

"Hi {{first_name}}, this is Sharath from gnani.ai. I’m following up after CCW Las Vegas. We work with contact-center and customer-operations teams on AI voice agents, agent assist, and conversation analytics. I wanted to quickly see whether improving call automation, agent productivity, or QA visibility is a priority for your team."

The app should avoid sounding like this:

"We are an AI chatbot company."

The pitch should always connect gnani.ai to a business problem.
