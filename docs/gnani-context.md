## 🧠 Proprietary Model Stack & Core Platforms

Unlike standard AI wrapper startups that rent third-party models (e.g., ElevenLabs + Vapi), Gnani.ai owns its entire speech and model stack from bare metal to agent orchestration:

### 1. **Gnani Artha™ (Sovereign AI Stack)**
* Officially launched in late August 2026 by the Vice President of India, C. P. Radhakrishnan.
* An end-to-end indigenous, sovereign AI platform allowing enterprises, banks, and government agencies to deploy state-of-the-art voice and reasoning agents entirely within private cloud or on-prem environments with zero data leakage.

### 2. **Evon v3.3 (Foundational LLM)**
* A **30-billion-parameter open-weights foundational Large Language Model (LLM)** built on a **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** architecture (activating ~3.5B parameters per token for ultra-fast, cost-efficient inference).
* Features a custom-engineered tokenizer specifically built for Indic scripts and languages (supporting 11 languages), drastically slashing token counts and inferencing costs compared to Western LLMs.
* Available with open weights on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license.

### 3. **Prisma v3.0 (Speech-to-Text Model)**
* Foundational STT model purpose-built for noisy, real-world telephonic voice audio.
* Explicitly engineered to master **code-mixed speech** (*Hinglish*, *Tamglish*, language switching mid-sentence) with **16.9% macro Word Error Rate (WER)** across 9 Indian languages and Indian English.

### 4. **Plexus™ (Agentic AI Orchestration Platform)**
* Enterprise agentic platform that connects Evon and Prisma models to live operational workflows, telephony SIP trunks, and core systems (LOS, CRM, ERP, EHR) to execute goal-driven autonomous journeys (KYC verification, claims filing, debt collection).

### 5. **Core Enterprise Suite**
* **Automate365™:** Agentic Voice AI for 70%+ routine call queue deflection at sub-200ms latency.
* **Assist365™:** Real-Time Agent Co-Pilot providing live rep guidance, script compliance, and instant knowledge retrieval during active calls.
* **Aura365™:** 100% Automated Call QA & Analytics auditing every single voice call for sentiment, tone, compliance, and intent.
* **Armour365™:** Voice Biometrics & Fraud Prevention using 300+ acoustic feature extraction for zero-trust caller identity verification.

## What I am selling

I am not trying to sell a generic chatbot.

I am selling business outcomes such as:

- reducing repetitive call volume (70%+ deflection)
- cutting cost-per-call from $6.50+ to under $0.80
- sub-200ms conversational latency (zero awkward AI lag)
- improving agent productivity and reducing onboarding time

## Main product angles

### 1. AI Voice Agents (Automate365)

AI voice agents can handle repetitive voice conversations, collect information, answer common questions, route calls, schedule callbacks, qualify leads, support customers, and hand off to human agents when needed.

Best-fit use cases:

- customer support
- appointment scheduling
- lead qualification
- payment reminders
- collections follow-up
- policy renewal
- claims status
- order status
- returns/refunds
- surveys
- onboarding
- customer engagement

### 2. Agent Assist

Agent Assist helps human agents during live calls.

It can provide:

- real-time suggestions
- next-best actions
- compliance reminders
- account/context guidance
- response suggestions
- post-call summaries
- faster documentation
- coaching insights

Best-fit use cases:

- contact centers
- BPOs
- healthcare enrollment teams
- insurance support
- financial services
- complex customer-service workflows
- new agent ramp-up
- QA and coaching

### 3. Conversation Analytics / QA Analytics

Conversation analytics helps teams understand what is happening across customer calls.

It can help with:

- QA coverage
- sentiment tracking
- compliance checks
- risk flagging
- coaching opportunities
- root-cause analysis
- customer pain discovery
- agent performance improvement

The pitch is: instead of reviewing only a small sample of calls manually, businesses can analyze customer conversations at scale.

### 4. Voice Biometrics

Voice biometrics helps businesses authenticate callers using voice.

It can help with:

- caller verification
- passive authentication
- fraud reduction
- faster authentication
- reduced dependence on security questions
- better customer experience
