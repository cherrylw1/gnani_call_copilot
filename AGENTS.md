### A. **Gnani Artha™ (Sovereign AI Stack)**
* Officially unveiled in late August 2026 by the **Vice President of India, C. P. Radhakrishnan**.
* Complete indigenous sovereign AI stack allowing enterprises, banks, and public institutions to run full speech and reasoning pipelines on private cloud or on-prem with complete data residency and zero external data leaks.

### B. **Evon v3.3 (Foundational LLM)**
* **30-billion-parameter open-weights foundational Large Language Model (LLM)** built on a **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** architecture (activating ~3.5 billion parameters per token for blazing speed and cost efficiency).
* Custom-engineered tokenizer built specifically for Indian scripts and languages (11 languages), slashing token counts and inferencing costs by over 40% compared to Western LLMs (Llama/GPT).
* Weights published on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0 license.

### C. **Prisma v3.0 (Speech-to-Text Model)**
* Foundational STT model purpose-built for noisy telephonic audio and regional code-mixed speech (*Hinglish*, *Tamglish*, fluid mid-sentence language switching).
* Delivers a record macro Word Error Rate (**WER**) of **16.9%** across 9 Indian languages + Indian English.

### D. **Plexus™ (Agentic AI Orchestration Platform)**
* Enterprise agent platform that binds Evon reasoning and Prisma transcription into live telephony (carrier SIP trunks) and core databases (LOS, CCaaS, CRM, EHR) to execute multi-turn, goal-driven autonomous journeys.

### E. **Core Product Suite**
